# Sonar

[![Status](https://flat.badgen.net/github/checks/cpulvermacher/sonar)](https://github.com/cpulvermacher/sonar/actions/workflows/node.js.yml)
[![License](https://flat.badgen.net/github/license/cpulvermacher/sonar)](./LICENSE)

Measure distances with your phone's speaker and microphone.

Just open
[https://cpulvermacher.github.io/sonar](https://cpulvermacher.github.io/sonar)
on your phone, press Start, allow microphone access and point it at a wall.

## Features

- Distance to the nearest wall-like reflector, from about 0.25 m to 2 m, several times per second
- Echogram of the last pings, and the echo profile of the latest one
- A map by compass direction, on devices with an orientation sensor

## Tips

- Turn the media volume up. The chirps are audible (2–12 kHz by default).
- If readings are off by a constant amount, adjust Offset: it is half the distance between your phone's speaker and its microphone.
- The speaker radiates in all directions. With nothing in front, the reading is the floor or the ceiling.
